## TICKET #1: Buses with inconsistent status

> **Problem:** Yesterday, two operators changed the same bus almost at the same time. One transferred it to MAINTENANCE and another to ON_ROUTE. The bus was left on ON_ROUTE, but the history shows two changes that don’t add up: AVAILABLE MAINTENANCE and AVAILABLE ON_ROUTE. In addition, a user that we deactivated on Monday was able to continue changing states until today.
